# Original Data Source

## Nigeria Geological Survey Agency Mineral Resources Map of Nigeria

The mineral occurrence records in this database were derived from the:

**Nigeria Geological Survey Agency (NGSA). (2023). _Mineral Resources Map of Nigeria_.**

The map is the principal source used for identifying mineral occurrence records included in the compiled database.

### Official NGSA Source

Official map:

https://ngsa.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Mineral_Resources_Map_of_Nigeria_2023.pdf

NGSA geological maps:

https://ngsa.gov.ng/geological-maps/

NGSA official website:

https://ngsa.gov.ng/

## Source Description

The NGSA Mineral Resources Map of Nigeria presents the geographical distribution of identified mineral occurrences across Nigeria.

The 2023 map incorporates mineral-location information generated by NGSA and updated through its geological and mineral-resource mapping activities.

## Use of the Source

The source map was used to:

1. identify mineral occurrence locations and associated mineral/resource codes;
2. compare occurrence records against an existing mineral resources workbook;
3. identify records absent from the existing workbook;
4. extract and structure the missing records;
5. retain source terminology and occurrence codes where applicable.

## Attribution

The original mineral occurrence information remains attributable to the **Nigeria Geological Survey Agency (NGSA)**.

This repository does not claim ownership of the original NGSA map, its cartographic design, or the underlying geological information represented by the source.

The contribution represented by this repository is the compilation, comparison, structuring, organisation, cleaning, documentation and publication of the resulting database.

## Recommended Source Citation

Nigeria Geological Survey Agency (NGSA). (2023). _Mineral Resources Map of Nigeria_.

## Important Note

This database is a secondary compilation derived from the NGSA source map. It should not be treated as an independent geological survey or as a replacement for official NGSA information.

Users requiring authoritative geological or mineral-resource information should consult the original NGSA publications and datasets.
